Profile

Research Journey

I am a Professor and Head of the Department of Computer Science and Engineering (IoT & Cybersecurity including Blockchain Technology) at Nagarjuna College of Engineering & Technology, Bengaluru, with over 20 years of dedicated teaching and research experience in Computer Science and Engineering.

My doctoral research at the National Institute of Technology (NIT), Tiruchirappalli investigated Blockchain-Based Smart Contract Security, formulating novel frameworks for automated vulnerability detection, digital forensics, and secure credit reporting. My broader research covers Blockchain, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, IoT, and Cybersecurity.

I have delivered 60+ Faculty Development Programmes, workshops, and invited technical sessions across premier institutions including multiple NITs and AICTE-sponsored programmes. I have also authored a university textbook on Data Communications & Networking (Bangalore University BCA IV Sem) and contribute actively as a reviewer, project mentor, and innovation facilitator.

Academic Objectives

  • Advance research in smart contract vulnerability detection and ML-driven security frameworks.
  • Pioneer curriculum development aligned with Outcome-Based Education (OBE) and NBA standards.
  • Mentor students in research, projects, and innovation to foster inquiry and technical excellence.
  • Strengthen industry–academia collaboration through joint research and applied problem-solving.
  • Expand national outreach through FDPs, workshops, and AICTE programmes.

Vision as HOD

  • Establish the department as a nationally recognized hub for IoT, Cybersecurity, and Blockchain education.
  • Cultivate a faculty culture of continuous learning, research publication, and pedagogical innovation.
  • Drive student-centred outcomes — employability, research aptitude, and entrepreneurial mindset.
  • Establish collaborative research labs and centres of excellence with industry partners.
  • Ensure rigorous Board of Studies governance and accreditation readiness.

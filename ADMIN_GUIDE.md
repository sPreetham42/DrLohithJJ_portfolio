# Non-Technical Administrator Guide for Dr. Lohith J.J.'s Portfolio

Welcome! This guide explains how to manage and update all content on your academic portfolio website. You do **not** need any coding knowledge, HTML, or technical software. All edits are made using clean forms in your web browser.

---

## 🔑 1. Logging Into Your Admin Dashboard (Sanity Studio)

1. Open your admin dashboard link in any browser (e.g. `https://dr-lohith-portfolio-studio.sanity.studio` or `http://localhost:3333` during local preview).
2. Log in using your email address and password.
3. You will see a left-hand navigation bar listing all your portfolio sections.

---

## 📚 2. How to Add a New Publication & Upload Paper PDF

1. In the left panel, click **Publications & Papers**.
2. Click the **`+ Create`** button at the top.
3. Fill out the simple form:
   - **Paper Title:** Type the full publication title.
   - **Authors List:** Type the author names (e.g., *L. J.J. and K. Singh*).
   - **Journal / Conference Name:** Type the venue name & volume details.
   - **Publication Type:** Choose *SCIE / Scopus Journal* or *IEEE Conference Proceeding*.
   - **Year:** Type the year (e.g., `2024`).
   - **DOI Link:** Paste the DOI link (e.g., `https://doi.org/10.1007/...`).
   - **Upload Full Paper PDF:** Click the **Upload** button and select the PDF file directly from your computer.
4. Click the green **Publish** button at the bottom right.

---

## 🏆 3. How to Add an Achievement or Award

1. Click **Achievements & Awards** in the left menu.
2. Click **`+ Create`**.
3. Type the **Award Title**, **Issuing Organization**, and **Year**.
4. (Optional) Click **Upload** to attach a certificate PDF.
5. Click **Publish**.

---

## 🎤 4. How to Add an Invited Talk or Workshop

1. Click **Invited Talks & Workshops** in the left menu.
2. Click **`+ Create`**.
3. Type the **Talk Topic / Title**, **Venue / College**, **Display Date Label** (e.g. `Feb 2026`), and **Filter Year** (e.g. `2026`).
4. Click **Publish**.

---

## 💼 5. How to Update Career Experience & Education

1. Click **Employment Experience** or **Education & Qualifications**.
2. Select an existing entry to edit, or click **`+ Create`** to add a new role or degree.
3. Update the fields and click **Publish**.

---

## 👤 6. How to Edit Your Bio or Profile Picture

1. Click **Profile & Bio (Hero)** at the top of the left menu.
2. Update your designation, intro paragraphs, phone number, or emails.
3. To change your profile picture: hover over the **Profile Photo** field, click **Replace**, and upload a new photo.
4. Click **Publish**.

---

## 🤖 7. Automatic Google Scholar Metrics Updates

Your website citations and h-index metrics are **automatically updated every night at 6:00 AM IST** directly from Google Scholar and OpenAlex. You do not need to take any action for metrics to stay updated!

---

## ❓ Summary of How Changes Appear Live

- Every time you click **Publish**, Sanity updates your website data automatically.
- Refresh your website page to view the updated content live.
